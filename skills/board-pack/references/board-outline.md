# Monthly board pack outline (10 slides plus appendix)

Titles are assertions that state the point of the slide, written from the approved figures.

| # | Slide | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Company, "Monthly results, <Month YYYY>", status FINAL or DRAFT | close_log.md |
| 2 | The month in one line | Headline sentence from approved commentary; 3 KPI tiles: revenue, EBITDA, cash | narrative, variance_summary |
| 3 | EBITDA bridge | Waterfall chart plan to actual; 2 bullets on the largest steps | bridge.csv |
| 4 | Revenue | Actual vs budget by entity chart; price, volume, mix split | variance.csv, pvm.csv |
| 5 | Margin and costs | Material COGS and opex variances with cause | material.csv |
| 6 | Cash and controls | Reconciliation status per entity, open breaks, journal flags count by severity | recon_summary, screening_summary |
| 7 | Full-year outlook | Fan chart; FY P10/P50/P90 for revenue and EBITDA; movement vs last forecast | forecast_summary |
| 8 | Risks and opportunities | Up to 3 each, sized, with owner | variance and forecast findings |
| 9 | Decisions needed | What the board is asked to decide or note | approved commentary |
| 10 | Pending sign-off | Only if any item is pending; otherwise omit | review_queue.csv |
| A1+ | Appendix | Entity P&Ls, full variance table, method notes | variance.xlsx |

Every slide carries a small footer: "Prepared with Orrery (educational tool). Draft for review by a qualified person." The cover also states that figures must be reviewed before use. In demo mode the cover says "Demo: fictional company and figures".

Charts: 1600x900, labelled axes with units, favourable green, adverse red, totals neutral.
