# Orrery house style for financial commentary

Override any rule here with the company's own style guide if one is provided.

## Voice

- Write like a CFO talking to peers: plain, direct, specific. No hype, no hedging chains.
- Active voice. "Price concessions on two renewals cost EUR 0.9M", not "A negative price variance was observed".
- Cause first, number second, line name last (or not at all).
- One idea per sentence. Most sentences under 25 words.
- Do not use em dashes. Use a full stop or a comma.

## Numbers

- Currency prefix once per paragraph, then numbers only: "EUR 12.8M, 0.4M ahead of plan".
- Millions with one decimal (12.8M), thousands with no decimals (240k), percentages with one decimal (3.4%).
- Favourable and adverse, not positive and negative. Say "ahead of" and "behind" plan.
- Always give the comparison base: "versus plan", "versus September".
- Never restate a table. Pick the two or three numbers that explain the result.

## Structure by audience

| Audience | Length | Content |
|---|---|---|
| CFO | Headline + up to 250 words | Result, top 3 drivers, outlook range, open items |
| Board | Headline + up to 150 words | Result, one driver paragraph, outlook range, decisions needed |
| Entity owners | Up to 120 words per entity | Their variances, the questions they must answer |

## Words to avoid

"Significant" (give the number), "headwinds/tailwinds" (name the cause), "timing" without evidence that it reverses, "one-off" for anything that happened twice in twelve months, "robust", "solid", "challenging".

## Citations

Square brackets after the figure: `[variance V003]`, `[bridge: Price]`, `[pvm DE-GmbH Gateway G5]`, `[forecast FY revenue P50]`, `[recon NL-BV]`. Every cited value must match `citations.csv` exactly before rounding.
